Dataplicity example container release notices
================================================

The Dataplicity-specific example workload in each release is distributed under
LICENSE.txt.

OCI archives also contain third-party operating-system packages and libraries,
including packages from Debian and, for compatible Raspberry Pi camera builds,
the Raspberry Pi package archive. Those components remain subject to their own
copyright notices and licence terms. The Dataplicity licence does not replace,
restrict, or relicense third-party components.

Copyright and licence metadata for installed Debian packages is available
inside the image under:

    /usr/share/doc/*/copyright

Corresponding source packages are available from the upstream distribution
repositories identified in the image package metadata:

    https://www.debian.org/distrib/packages
    https://archive.raspberrypi.com/debian/

FFmpeg and its linked libraries are distributed under their applicable
LGPL/GPL terms as documented by the installed package and upstream project:

    https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

Raspberry Pi camera packages and libcamera retain their upstream terms:

    https://github.com/raspberrypi/rpicam-apps
    https://libcamera.org/

Release-specific software inventories should be generated from the OCI archive
by the publishing workflow and attached as SBOM/provenance metadata where
supported.
